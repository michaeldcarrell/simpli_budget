Everything a monthly budget needs

Bank sync

Link your accounts once through Plaid, and new transactions arrive every night on their own.

Automatic categories

Rules sort transactions as they come in, matching on the name ("starts with Shell") or the amount.

The month at a glance

Income, spending and what's left up top, then every category against its budget, with overspending flagged.

Budgets that roll forward

Give each category a default monthly amount, then adjust any single month without touching the rest.

Spending alerts

Pick the categories you care about and get a summary by Discord or email, as often as you like.

Built for your phone

The same budget on any screen, with a tab bar on mobile and light and dark themes.

Transactions

Find any purchase in seconds

Every transaction from every account in one list, grouped by day. Search by name, filter by date, category, account or tag, jump straight to what still needs a category, and export what you find.

Categories

Open any category to see what you spent, what you budgeted, and the transactions that got you there. Recategorize one right from the list, and tag transactions to follow things like a trip across categories.

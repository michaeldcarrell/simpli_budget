Settings

Used to send you your daily budget notification categories summary as a Discord DM.
How to find your Discord User ID:
  1. In Discord, open Settings → Advanced, and turn on Developer Mode.
  2. Right-click (or tap and hold, on mobile) your name or avatar anywhere in Discord.
  3. Click Copy User ID and paste it into the field above.
It's a long number (e.g. 123456789012345678), not your username.
